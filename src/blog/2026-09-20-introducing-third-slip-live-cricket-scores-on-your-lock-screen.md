I've just launched a new app: [Third Slip](https://thirdslip.app), available on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/third-slip-cricket-scores/id6804360322). It's an app for cricket fans to follow the game with live scores, but, most importantly, it puts those scores on your Lock Screen. It uses Live Activities on your iPhone to surface the score everywhere it matters: on your Lock Screen, in the Dynamic Island, on your Apple Watch, and even in your car through CarPlay.

![An iPhone Lock Screen showing Third Slip's live scores for two cricket matches.](/images/e43c5333-9ab1-46e0-bdef-d5d31f72863e/third_slip_screenshot.png)

*Cricket scores on your lock screen!*

Earlier this year, I [launched an app](https://zachsim.one/blog/2026/3/1/introducing-six-again-nrl-scores-on-your-lock-screen) called [Six Again](https://sixagain.app), which does something similar for NRL (rugby league) scores. In Six Again's [launch post](https://zachsim.one/blog/2026/3/1/introducing-six-again-nrl-scores-on-your-lock-screen), I explain a lifelong obsession with checking the NRL scores. The sense of urgency certainly isn't the same when it comes to cricket scores, but it's safe to say that once October hits, the footy season is done and dusted (another year where the Dragons don't win, probably), and the weather warms up, I turn into an almost equally obsessed cricket fan. The week between Christmas and New Year is for watching the Boxing Day Test, obviously. As is the first week of the New Year with the Sydney Test. All while the [BBL](https://www.bigbash.com.au) is going on through until the end of January. But of course, practically speaking, it's impossible to watch _all_ that cricket. You're probably sneaking glances in amongst other things. So you need an app to follow along, and that's exactly where Third Slip comes in. It's the perfect companion for keeping up with the scores when you can't watch the cricket live.

Third Slip has everything a cricket fan needs to follow along: live scores, scorecards, ball-by-ball action, tables and rankings, team lists, and the latest cricket news. Its coverage is broad, with all major Tests, ODIs, and T20s covered, as well as all the major competitions you'd expect, such as the BBL, the IPL, and The Hundred - across men's and women's competitions. Where possible, if we have a men's league, we have the equivalent women's league.

The Live Activities are the standout feature, but the app is also fast, clean, and easy to use. You can follow your favourite teams and leagues, and the app will surface relevant matches to you.

Most of the app is free to use. Your first 3 Live Activities are also free, but after that a subscription is required. For that, you get auto-starting Live Activities for any team or league you follow.

Third Slip is available on the [iOS App Store](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/third-slip-cricket-scores/id6804360322) to start with. As with Six Again, I expect an Android version to follow shortly but don't have an estimated release date just yet.

Third Slip is the cricket app with Live Activities that I've been hoping for since the Live Activities feature first came to the iPhone. I've enjoyed making it, and love using it. If you're into cricket, I hope you check it out, and that you love it as well. If you do, tell a friend! I'd be open to any feedback you have. There's a Contact button in the app - please use it to let me know what you think!

Third Slip is available on the App Store at [this link](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/third-slip-cricket-scores/id6804360322).
