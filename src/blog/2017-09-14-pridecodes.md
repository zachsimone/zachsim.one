If you've visited the [main page](https://zachsim.one) of my site in the last week or so, you would've noticed a new banner in the top right-hand corner.

![Image 1.png](/images/1505359593788-HOK9WZ23M1S8VKGT388E/Image-1.png)

This clean, simple "Vote Yes" banner was designed and developed by [Pat Murray](https://twitter.com/_patmurray) and [Jack Skinner](https://twitter.com/developerjack), and is part of many banners available for website owners to implement, as a part of their [Pride.codes](https://pride.codes) project.

![Image 2.png](/images/1505360092425-8V0C3WSYQ8PYXOJ4Y7SF/Image-2.png)

As Australians begin to receive their voting forms for the same-sex marriage survey, these banners are a nice way to show support for the LGBTQI+ community.

Some further examples include the front page of [The Sizzle](https://thesizzle.com.au), [Jake Araullo's site](http://jaraullo.com), and [Pat's site](http://patmurraydev.com).

The project is open source, and you can [contribute on GitHub](https://github.com/devjack/pride.codes) should you wish, or follow the [Twitter account](https://twitter.com/pridecodes) for updates.

If you're able, and would like to show your support, I'd encourage you to check out [pride.codes](https://pride.codes), and add a banner to your site too.
