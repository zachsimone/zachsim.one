[Petty](https://petty-app.com) is making a return to Android! 

Petty is my fuel price finder app, helping you find stations nearby and check the price before you decide to fill up. It works in most states across Australia and the United Kingdom. 

![Petty for Android](/images/1791970363000/petty-android.png)

When [Petty 1.0 launched](https://zachsim.one/blog/2017/7/27/introducing-petty) in July 2017, it launched on both iOS and Android. At some point along the way, I stopped updating it for Android. The iOS version kept moving forward, [with](https://zachsim.one/blog/2019/2/21/petty-21) [tonnes](https://zachsim.one/blog/2020/8/4/petty-31) [of](https://zachsim.one/blog/2020/9/19/introducing-petty-40) [great](https://zachsim.one/blog/2024/2/1/petty-70-introducing-carplay) [features](https://zachsim.one/blog/2024/3/5/petty-71-tomorrow-prices-wa) [and](https://zachsim.one/blog/2026/2/10/petty-80-now-with-uk-fuel-prices) [additions](https://zachsim.one/blog/2026/5/1/petty-81). Reviving the Android app has always been in the back of my mind.

Well, today it has happened, and Petty is once again [available on Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=co.harbourcity.petty). It is a new listing on Google Play, requiring a fresh download rather than an update to the original version.

If you've been looking for a good fuel prices app to use on Android, Petty's got you covered. 

You can use Petty on Android to find fuel stations nearby, check the latest prices, view station price history, mark your favourites, and view price trends. You can also calculate how much you'll save at one station compared to another, and manage multiple vehicles with different fuel types.

As always, please pass on any feedback or suggestions. I'm always happy to hear from you! The best way to get in touch is using the Contact button in the app's Settings.

Petty for Android is available to download on Google Play: [Download Petty](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=co.harbourcity.petty).
