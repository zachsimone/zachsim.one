Applications closed 24 hours ago for [student scholarships](https://developer.apple.com/wwdc/scholarships/) to Apple’s World Wide Developers Conference ([WWDC](https://developer.apple.com/wwdc/)). For the first time, I’ve sent in a submission. Having only ever heard positive things about WWDC, it is a dream to attend one day, even if that day doesn’t occur this year. 

By this time next week, the status of my application should be known. Undoubtedly the first place I’ll share the news (should I be accepted or rejected) will be [Twitter](http://twitter.com/zachsimone). Now the wait begins…
