# About

I'm a iOS developer from Sydney, Australia. I work on my own projects, most of which are available on the App Store. You can check out my published apps [here](/projects). Most of my professional work is contract work for various clients - I've worked on a variety of projects for companies large and small. Feel free to [get in touch through my company website](https://harbourcitysoftware.com.au) if you think I might be a good fit to help with one of your projects.

I help organise the [Sydney CocoaHeads](https://www.meetup.com/sydneycocoaheads) meetup, and (used to) co-host the [Cup of Tech](http://cupof.tech) podcast.

Technically you can still contact me on [X/Twitter](https://twitter.com/zachsimone), but [Mastodon](https://mastodon.social/@zachsimone) seems to be the better place to do that now. Otherwise, feel free to send an [email](/contact).
